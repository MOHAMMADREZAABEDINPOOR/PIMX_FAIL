## ⚡ Project Overview

Survivorship bias blinds entrepreneurs to the reality that over 90% of technology startups collapse. Most books highlight rare unicorns while ignoring the cemetery of failed ventures.

**PIMX_FAIL** inverts this narrative:
- 💀 **Empirical Cemetery Analytics**: Documents hundreds of real-world post-mortems with hard financial figures and timelines.
- 🔍 **Root-Cause Tagging**: Categorizes failures by premature scaling, toxic unit economics, regulatory shutdown, and hiring mismanagement.
- 🛡️ **Defensive Strategy Simulator**: Interactive lessons helping builders spot early fatal signals before running out of runway.
